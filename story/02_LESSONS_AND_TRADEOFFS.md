## Lesson 1 · P1 Incidents Span All 10 Queues (Do Not Gate on Queue Name Alone)
- **Outcome:** In `kb_train.parquet` (N = 16,000), `2,824` tickets (`17.65%`) are critical P1 (`priority == "high" AND type == "Incident"`). Only `231` (`8.2%`) live in `Service Outages and Maintenance`; `1,490` (`52.8%`) route to `Technical Support`, `415` (`14.7%`) to `Product Support`, and `348` (`12.3%`) to `IT Support`.
- **Source:** Observed directly in [`artifacts/kb_audit_summary.json`](../artifacts/kb_audit_summary.json) generated by [`backend/data_prep.py`](../backend/data_prep.py).
- **Transfer Conditions:** Applies to any enterprise multi-queue ITIL helpdesk where outage tickets carry specialized subsystem queues (network, encryption, product, IT). Recheck if an organization uses a dedicated P1-only queue enforced upstream.
- **Next Test:** Measure whether routing P1 `Technical Support` vs `IT Support` tickets benefits from live `gemini-decision-1` Pass-1 blending once `GEMINI_API_KEY` is configured.

## Lesson 2 · `google/embeddinggemma-2` Text-Only Loading Still Imports Vision Processor Dependencies
- **Outcome:** Initializing `SentenceTransformer("google/embeddinggemma-2", config_kwargs={"vision_config": None, "audio_config": None})` on PyTorch `cu128` succeeded in downloading all 413 model weights, but `AutoProcessor` raised missing optional dependency errors until both `pillow` and `torchvision` were installed in `.venv`. With both installed, `google/embeddinggemma-2` ran natively on the RTX 5070 in `torch.bfloat16` (`83.23s` for 16,000 docs, 0 NaNs, exact unit L2 norms, 256d Matryoshka).
- **Source:** Direct execution logs of [`backend/indexing.py`](../backend/indexing.py) and [`artifacts/index_meta.json`](../artifacts/index_meta.json).
- **Transfer Conditions:** Applies to `sentence-transformers>=6.1.0` + `transformers>=5.x` environments loading multimodal-capable Gemma embedding checkpoints in text-only mode. Always pin `pillow` and `torchvision` in `requirements.txt` and keep an automatic fallback (`intfloat/multilingual-e5-small`).
- **Next Test:** Verify clean cold-start setup on a fresh CPU-only container using `requirements.txt` to confirm `float32` fallback path stays NaN-free.

## Lesson 3 · Decouple Calibrated Probabilities (ECE) from Imbalanced Argmax Decoding (Macro-F1)
- **Outcome:** Across 10 queues with a `21.7×` class imbalance (`Technical Support` 29.34% vs. `General Inquiry` 1.35%), standard calibrated argmax yielded low ECE (`0.0148`) but suppressed recall on rare queues (`Sales and Pre-Sales` 7.5%, `General Inquiry` 4.3%). Applying Menon-style prior-adjusted decoding (`p_i / prior_i^alpha`, with `alpha` tuned strictly on the internal 15% `kb_train` validation carve-out) improves rare-queue Macro-F1 while leaving raw calibrated probabilities untouched for ECE and escalation threshold gating.
- **Source:** Controlled experiment in [`backend/train_ml.py`](../backend/train_ml.py) and [`backend/features.py`](../backend/features.py), evaluated on `holdout_test.parquet` via [`evaluate.py`](../evaluate.py).
- **Transfer Conditions:** Applies whenever a classifier serves both human-facing calibration thresholds (requiring true posteriors) and imbalanced multi-class Macro-F1 routing. Recheck if downstream consumers assume `argmax(probabilities) == predicted_label` without exposing `prior_alpha`.
- **Next Test:** Compare prior-adjusted local ML against `0.55 ML + 0.45 gemini-decision-1` on the rare queues (`General Inquiry`, `Human Resources`, `Sales and Pre-Sales`) with a live `GEMINI_API_KEY`.

## Lesson 4 · Safety-Margin Recall Tuning & Non-High Priority Gating Prevent False Alert Fatigue
- **Outcome:** Targeting `92%` P1 recall on the internal 2,400-row validation split yielded `89.17%` on the unseen 4,000-row holdout due to finite-sample variance; raising the internal validation target to `95%` (still strictly inside `kb_train`) provides the safety margin required to stay `>= 90%` out-of-sample. Simultaneously, evaluating Rule #2 low-confidence gating on `P(not high)` for routine non-Incident tickets avoids needlessly escalating crystal-clear billing/info requests where `low` vs `medium` is split 54/46.
- **Source:** Holdout evaluation runs of [`evaluate.py`](../evaluate.py) and adversarial case `ADV-08`.
- **Transfer Conditions:** Applies to multi-class operational triage systems combining strict P1 recall SLAs with noisy human `low`/`medium` priority annotations. Recheck if SLA contracts impose strict differentiation between `low` and `medium` turnaround times.
- **Next Test:** Review false-positive escalation rate over a 100-ticket live operator shadow shift to tune `MIN_CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD` (`0.65`) per queue.
